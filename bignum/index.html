bignum

Can a local model do long arithmetic in its head, and what happens when models write programs instead? A reproduction and extension of @simonw's Qwen3.8 "addition in words" experiment.

Report

  • Results: findings, tables, reproduction, error anatomy, hardware.

Experiments

  • In-head arithmetic: addition and multiplication in words, and factoring, answered in the head: no thinking vs thinking, on two local Qwen models.
  • SOTA factorization: Sonnet and Opus each get an hour to write the fastest factoring program they can; what they built, how long it took, and how far it reaches.
