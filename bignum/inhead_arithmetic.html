In-head arithmetic

Created on 6 October 2026 by Claude Opus 5.5, prompted by @cristipp127.

Answering in the head (no thinking vs thinking) on add + words, mul + words and factoring. Every finished run, by digit-length cell. Each panel shows one run as a grid in @simonw's style, starting from its own task selector. The x axis is the digits of a (or p), and the y axis is the digits of b (or q). The overview at the bottom plots accuracy against total time for every run of a task. The page refreshes itself as new results arrive, and its state is kept in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a comparison.

Set a panel’s “clone from” to another panel to make it follow all of that panel’s selections (chains allowed); change a selector there to override just that field. Clone a panel and change one selector to compare. The view is kept in the link, so an old link reopens an old view; “Reset to default view” below brings back the default.

Overview: accuracy vs time to completion

Click a point to open it as a new panel. Ringed points (#n) are shown in panels.

Time is hardware-dependent. @simonw's runs: NVIDIA DGX Spark (GB10), llama.cpp (CUDA), Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M, about 12 tok/s. @cristipp127's: MacBook Pro with Apple M5 Max (128 GB), oMLX, oQ4e weights with multi-token prediction, about 30 tok/s on the 27B and 77 tok/s on Flash. Token counts are about the same, so the time gap is the setup, not the model. Our runs all share the same machine and compare fairly with each other.
