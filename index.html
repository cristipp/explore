explore

Small research projects, each with its report, interactive pages and the code or data behind it.

  • bignum: can a local model do long arithmetic in its head, and what happens when models write programs instead? A reproduction and extension of @simonw's Qwen3.8 "addition in words" experiment, plus an hour of Sonnet and Opus writing the fastest factoring program they can.
  • foresee: can you know what a program will do without running it? @stephen_wolfram's two sentences on foreseeing programs, made precise.
  • singularity: what happens to a fleet of spacecraft that falls into a black hole at an angle? Exact geodesics, integrated live: the fleet is squeezed into a thread wound around the hole, not a needle pointing at it.
